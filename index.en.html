折り紙の動物園 (Origami Zoo)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

This app does not collect any of your information.

折り紙の動物園 (Origami Zoo; the “App”) is designed with your privacy as the most important thing. The “diary of words folded into paper” at the heart of the App’s experience is yours alone, and no one but you — including the developer — can see it.

What We Do Not Collect

  • All data stays only on your device. The animals you folded, the words you wrote on the paper, the dates and the App’s settings are stored only in this device’s local storage.
  • No network communication. The App does not connect to the internet and does not send data to any server.
  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • No analytics or tracking. No usage data collection, advertising, or third-party analytics tools or SDKs are built in.

Notifications

All notifications are “local notifications” handled entirely on the device. You can choose whether to enable notifications. Neither the content of notifications nor your usage is sent anywhere outside the device.

Backups and Switching Devices

The words you wrote are included in the iPhone’s standard backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer) in the same way as other apps’ data. This is your own backup, and the developer cannot access it. When you switch devices, restoring from a backup carries your words and animals over to the new device.

Deleting Data

If you delete the App from the device, all data on the device (the animals you folded and the words you wrote) is deleted with it. This cannot be undone.
